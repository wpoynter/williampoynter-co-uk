I am an engineering leader and independent technical consultant with 15 years’ experience across backend development, data architecture and technical leadership. I started as a keen hobbyist, teaching myself to write software at university in the PHP-and-MySQL era. Early roles often meant being the most senior engineer, sometimes the only one, and developing the full stack. I have always been drawn most strongly to backend, systems and data problems.

My work has particular depth in healthcare, research and data-rich products. I have built systems from the ground up, led platform, security and compliance functions, and grown engineering organisations to more than 70 people. I enjoy building great teams: models such as Agile, tribes and squads are useful, but the people in a team always matter more than the model.

I focus on product development, technical strategy and helping teams adopt AI thoughtfully. I co-founded ResearchWiseAI, a platform that automates survey-data analysis and reporting, and work with organisations on architecture, team building and delivery.

Outside work, I keep moving: I have completed around 20 half marathons and cycled Land’s End to John o’ Groats. I am also a PADI scuba diver and RYA Day Skipper.
