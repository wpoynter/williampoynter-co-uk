In our latest ResearchWiseAI Talking AI episode, Ray and I delve into the evolving role of AI agents in market research. We explore the distinctions between autonomous agents, like Roomba and Grammarly, and reactive agents powered by advanced large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT Operator.
